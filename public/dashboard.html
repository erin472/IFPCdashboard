G.E.A.R. Impact: Before vs After

Guidewheel was installed at IFPC. The first two weeks of available data are used as a baseline representing typical performance before Guidewheel-driven improvements were implemented. The current period defaults to the most recent two weeks of data and is compared against that baseline to quantify the impact of visibility and continuous-improvement work enabled by Guidewheel.

Plant-Wide $ Impact Assumptions
Plant Recovered Hrs/Year = (Plant Runtime Δ pp ÷ 100) × Operating Hrs/Year  ·  Recovered Production $ = Plant Recovered Hrs × Plant Throughput × Price/lb.  Operating Hrs/Year defaults to 8760 (24/7); use 6000 for 2 shifts × 5 days, etc.

Runtime vs Energy: Equipment Group Comparison

Bubble = Total kWh

Each bubble is one equipment group. X-axis = total runtime hours, Y-axis = total energy (kWh). Larger bubbles = more total energy. Groups in the upper-left are consuming high energy for low runtime — potential inefficiency.

Normalized Comparison

For each week, shows what % of total runtime and % of total energy each group consumed. If a group's energy share is much larger than its runtime share, it's less efficient.
